The UKRI/ERC Project *Class Struggle in Ancient Greek Democracy* presents a roundtable discussion on **Class, Feudal and Tributary Modes of Production, and the Ancient Greek Economy**, held in memory of Professor Chris Wickham.

This event introduces an [all-day workshop on Class and the Economy](/ClassDem/blog/2026/11/13/class-economy-workshop/) held on the following day, bringing together leading scholars to explore the dynamics of economic structures and class formation in ancient Greek poleis.

## Event Details

**Date:** 12 November 2026  
**Time:** 17:15–19:00  
**Location:** Usha Kasera Lecture Theatre, Old College, University of Edinburgh

The roundtable will be introduced and chaired by **Mirko Canevaro** and **David Lewis**.

The discussion will feature contributions from:

- **Alain Bresson** (University of Chicago)
- **Michele Faraguna** (University of Trieste)

- **Stephan Nitu** (University of Edinburgh)
- **Maeve McHugh** (University of Edinburgh)
- **Julien Zurbach** (École Normale Supérieure)

## About the Event

This roundtable addresses fundamental questions about the applicability of concepts such as feudal and tributary modes of production to ancient Greek economies. The discussion will explore how class dynamics, grounded in economic relations and occupational structures, shaped the emergence and development of Greek democracy.

The event is held in memory of **Professor Chris Wickham**, whose work on comparative economic history and modes of production has profoundly influenced our understanding of pre-modern societies.
