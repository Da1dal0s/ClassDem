## About the Workshop

This workshop forms the first major research gathering for the project's strand on **Class and the Economy**. Papers will address how occupational structures, conditions of labour, and economic life shaped class identities and class struggle in Greek democracies.

Key themes include:
- The occupational structure of Greek cities and its role in class formation
- Labour relations and conditions of dependency
- Economic complexity and redistributive mechanisms
- Exploitation and resistance in ancient economic systems

The workshop aims to develop a more integrated understanding of how economic structures underpinned the political and social dynamics of Greek democracy.
